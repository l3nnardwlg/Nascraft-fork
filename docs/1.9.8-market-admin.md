# Nascraft 1.9.8 Market Admin

`/market admin` requires `nascraft.market.admin`.

The dashboard contains three primary actions:

- Lower market prices
- Edit market
- Raise market prices

The market editor supports drag-and-drop item creation, item/category editing, price/property editing, and per-item trading availability.

In the market editor:

- Shift + Left Click an item toggles whether players can buy it.
- Shift + Right Click an item toggles whether players can sell it.

Availability is persisted in `items.yml` using `buy-enabled` and `sell-enabled` and is enforced centrally through Nascraft buy/sell events.

The price manipulation menus apply 1%, 5%, 10%, or 25% changes to all parent market items and persist the resulting item state immediately.
